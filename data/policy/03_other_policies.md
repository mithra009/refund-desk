# Other store policies

These clauses exist so retrieval has distractors. None of them changes the outcome of the
claim types handled by the refund agent.

## R14 — Refund method
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Store credit is offered only when the original
method is closed.

## R15 — Processing time
Refunds are processed within 5 business days of approval. Banks may take longer to show them.

## R16 — Gift cards
Gift cards are not refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## R17 — International orders
Customs duties and import taxes collected by the carrier are not refunded by the store.

## R18 — Subscriptions
Subscription renewals can be cancelled at any time; the current period is not prorated.

## R19 — Loyalty points
Points earned on a refunded order are removed from the customer's balance.

## R20 — Price matching
Price match requests must be made before purchase. Post-purchase price adjustments are not offered.

## R21 — Manufacturer warranty
Faults that appear after 30 days of normal use are handled under the manufacturer's warranty, not
by the store.

## R22 — Bundles
Items sold as a bundle are priced and refunded as separate items at their listed prices.

## R23 — Pre-orders
Pre-orders can be cancelled for a full refund until the item ships.

## R24 — Order changes
Orders can be changed or cancelled only until they are handed to the carrier.

## R25 — Customer communication
Replies to customers are polite and brief. They state the decision and the amount, say when the
money will arrive, and never quote internal rule numbers, risk assessments or staff notes.
